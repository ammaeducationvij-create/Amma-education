About Our Institute

Dedicated to Empowering Youth Through Practical Vocational Skills

Amma Education is dedicated to providing quality skill development and vocational training opportunities to students from rural and semi-urban communities under the flagship Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY).

Our mission is to help students improve their communication skills, soft skills, computer knowledge, and technical abilities to prepare them thoroughly for professional careers in modern industries.

We aim to create a supportive learning environment where students can learn new skills without worrying about training expenses, accommodation, or food. We also support students with career guidance, interview preparation, and placement assistance after successful completion of their training.

🌟

Supportive, Worry-Free Learning Environment

Students focus purely on learning, fully relieved of financial pressure regarding fees, boarding, or lodging.

💻

Modern Practical Lab Infrastructure

Hands-on computer systems, high-speed internet, projector classrooms, and interactive speaking labs.

🎯

Comprehensive Career Guidance & Grooming

Dedicated personality development, resume building, mock interviews, and corporate connections.
